I am currently a second year computer science PhD working with Prof. Prashant Pandey at Northeastern University DATA Lab started Fall 2025.

I'm the lead developer of Jasper, a GPU library for billion-scale approximate nearest neighbor search that outperforms NVIDIA cuVS on both index construction and query throughput with a smaller GPU memory footprint.

Before this, I worked on building adaptive state management for streaming system at CASP Lab at Boston University.
